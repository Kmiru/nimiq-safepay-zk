SafePay ZK

Verify before you pay.

SafePay ZK is a Mini App concept for Nimiq Pay that adds a verification layer before payment. A business creates a payment request, the user scans it, reviews it, verifies it, and pays through Nimiq Pay.

What it does

A safer payment flow before sending NIM

01

Create payment requests

Business connects with Nimiq Pay, creates an order, and generates a SafePay QR code.

02

Scan and review

Personal scans the QR code and reviews recipient, amount, network, expiration, and order details.

03

Verify before payment

SafePay verifies the payment request before enabling the PAY action.

04

Pay with Nimiq Pay

Nimiq Pay still handles the wallet, user confirmation, and transaction.

05

Attach SafePay data

The payment includes SafePay intent data, allowing Business to detect the matching payment on-chain.

06

Detect Paid / Expired

While Business is open, SafePay checks the Nimiq testnet and updates orders as Paid or Expired.

Architecture

Testnet + blockchain-only + auto-check

The current prototype does not use a backend. SafePay ZK creates local payment requests and uses the Nimiq testnet blockchain as the payment source of truth. When Business is open, the app checks the blockchain and updates order status based on matching transactions.

  • No backend yet
  • No custody of funds
  • Nimiq Pay handles wallet confirmation and payment
  • SafePay adds verification before payment
  • Business detects Paid / Expired while the app is open
Roadmap

Next phases

Phase 1

Competition prototype polish

Keep the testnet prototype stable, polish Business and Personal flows, improve receipts, and add anti-self-payment checks.

Phase 2

Real ZK verification

Connect the ZK proof to each real payment request, remove the fixed demo proof, generate a unique intentHash per order, and verify before PAY.

Phase 3

Mainnet preparation

Add strict network validation, separate testnet/mainnet storage, block self-payments, and test with very small real payments.

Phase 4

Backend or indexer

Add backend or indexer support so Business can update order status even when the device is closed.

Phase 5

Production hardening

Review the ZK circuit, audit the flow, improve logs and error handling, add privacy documentation, and prepare for real-world use.
